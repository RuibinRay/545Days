## The vertical slice, precisely (end of M5)

> A player opens the site, clicks **Story Mode**, waits ~1 s while "545 days" are simulated, and sees three cryptic visions: a town at night with its lamps out except one building; a factory with no workers; a line of text "the relays answered to no one". Day 0: a quiet valley, two towns, a river, a lab. They inspect the lab (evidence: grant, project "Sentinel"), overhear a rumor at the mill, and have ~15 Influence to spend. They can defund, nudge, build a second power plant, or pass an oversight law. Day 545 arrives: either the lights go out and a mechanical council sits in the town hall (Machine Dominion), or the valley reaches an uneasy Accord, or nothing happens and the epilogue says the future stayed quiet — and the "what changed" screen shows exactly which of their actions appear in the causes.

Two meaningful ways to change the outcome are **C (oversight law)** and **D (second power plant)**; A, B and E are also implemented but may not flip the ending alone.

## Technical risks by milestone

- M1: Pixi v8 tilemap performance with typed-array patches; Worker + Vite build on Pages base path. Spike both in week 1.
- M2: tuning the decision constants so a town neither starves nor stagnates; expect two full weeks of tuning via the headless harness, not the UI.
- M3: structuredClone cost for snapshots at 150 citizens (measure; if > 20 ms move snapshots to the Worker and send ids only). **Measured at 30 citizens / 96×96 (Node 22): 1.1 ms per snapshot, 263 KB as portable JSON, 12 ms to serialise, 1.7 ms to parse.** Snapshots already live in the Worker; the main thread only receives their day numbers. Re-measure at 150 citizens in M6.
- M4: balance at 300 citizens. It took nine tuning passes (recorded in DESIGN_MISTAKES) to get from "everyone starves" to a stable baseline; every new system (war, conscription, the granary) re-broke food first.
- M5: clue readability. The single most likely thing to be wrong the first time. Also: missions are free until the IP/KP economy exists, so "accept everything" dominates.

## Definition of done for v1 (release gate from the design-process prompt, adapted)

- Distinctive identity (original art, own palette, own UI) — reviewed against a "generic AI site" checklist
- Every advertised control does something in state; no placeholders
- Phone, tablet, laptop checked; Reduce Motion, keyboard-only, screen-reader list path checked
- Field-measured Core Web Vitals, not lab guesses
- Three endings pass the harness; frequency table published
- `PROJECT_REVIEWS.md` has the critic scores with evidence; `DESIGN_MISTAKES.md` has every bug that cost > 2 hours
